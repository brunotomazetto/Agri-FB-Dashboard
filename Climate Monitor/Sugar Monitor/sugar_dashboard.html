Source: NASA POWER / ERA5 (selectable model, historical) + Open-Meteo (observed, to bridge the gap since the last historical refresh). Observed data only — no forecast: the series ends on the last day that has actually happened. Always the current year vs. the distribution of recent years. Thick line = current year · thin = individual years · shading = min–max range · dashed = mean. My mills: your own clusters are stored in your account, not in this browser — signing out or clearing the cache does not lose them, and they stay until you delete them. Only you can see them. Each point snaps to the NASA POWER grid cell (0.5° × 0.625°, ~55 × 67 km) that contains it, so two points less than ~50 km apart legitimately share the same series. Temperature = monthly/weekly mean · Rainfall = monthly/weekly total. Mill location is a proxy for the surrounding cane-supply region. A multi-mill average has a naturally narrow band (spatial cancellation) — select a single mill to see the real spread.
